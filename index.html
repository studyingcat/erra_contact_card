My name is Nurshaherra Binti Bohri, and I am a Multimedia Computing student. For my Final Year Project, I developed MoMo: A Regression-based AI Approach for Money Monitoring Application with Predictive Spending Analytics.

MoMo is an Android money monitoring application that helps young adults manage their spending through budget planning, expense tracking, and future spending prediction. The project aims to solve the problem where users often record expenses only after spending, without knowing their possible future spending risks. As a solution, MoMo uses linear regression to analyse past spending patterns and predict next-month spending, helping users become more aware and make better financial decisions before overspending happens.
